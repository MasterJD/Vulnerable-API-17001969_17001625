## What is Cyclomatic Complexity?

Cyclomatic complexity measures the number of independent execution paths through a function. Each decision point (`if`, `else`, `for`, `while`, `switch/case`, `catch`, `&&`, `||`, `? :`) adds +1 to the base complexity of 1.

| Complexity | Risk Level | Interpretation |
|------------|------------|----------------|
| 1–5        | Low        | Simple, easy to test and maintain |
| 6–10       | Moderate   | Acceptable; may benefit from refactoring |
| 11–20      | High       | Difficult to test; refactoring recommended |
| 21+        | Very High  | Untestable; must be refactored |
